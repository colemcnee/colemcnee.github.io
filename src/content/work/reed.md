A synth where the shape and the sound are the same object — the oscillator reads a warped sphere, a
needle takes one lap across the surface, and the height under the needle is the waveform. Six
controls warp the surface and two move the needle, so sweeping from the equator toward a pole morphs
the tone continuously without touching a single warp, which is the thing a flat curve could not
offer. Nothing on screen is decoration, and the bench proves it by sweeping every control across its
range and measuring the spectrum at both ends.
