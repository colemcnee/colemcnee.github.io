Proof's sibling, with one architectural difference that is the whole point: there every layer works
from its own number and the cycles never line up, so the parts drift apart forever and it sounds
like weather. Here there is a conductor — a key, a progression, a bar count, a section — and every
part asks it what is happening rather than working it out alone, which is what makes it a song
rather than a texture. An audition mode writes two hundred songs and scores them, honest that it can
cull the ones carrying a flat defect but that ordering the survivors is guesswork.
