Add the chores that never make it onto a list — wipe the counters, change the sheets, vacuum the
couch — and get reminded when they are actually due. A chore's next due date is when you last
actually did it plus its interval, so skipping vacuuming for two weeks makes it overdue rather than
stacking up a backlog of missed cycles. A todo list that punishes you for a bad week gets deleted
after a bad week.
