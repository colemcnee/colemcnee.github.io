An action-adventure-rhythm game made with Sam Mills in Godot, about an aardvark whose lunch gets
stolen. You move through scenes — waking up, a convenience store, a pinball machine, a wall of death
— and the encounters resolve as timing minigames rather than combat. The scene names are more or
less the design document.
