Made with Sam Mills in Godot. A rhythm game wearing an action-adventure coat: you move through
scenes — waking up, the convenience store, a pinball machine, a wall of death — and the
encounters resolve as timing minigames rather than combat.

The scene names are the design document, more or less. *The Scene Where Baardvark Wakes Up*,
then *The Scene Where Baardvark's Lunch Is Stolen*, and everything after that is trying to get
it back.
